ECCV 2026

Defect-Aware Hybrid Prompt Optimization for Zero-Shot Multi-Type Anomaly Detection and Segmentation

Nadeem Nazer*, Hongkuan Zhou*, Lavdim Halilaj, Ylli Sadikaj, Steffen Staab

European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV), 2026

arXiv Proceedings Code

DAPO adds fine-grained defect semantics such as hole, bent or scratch to CLIP-based anomaly detection. Its hybrid prompts pair human-readable defect descriptions with learnable tokens. Under distribution shift it improves image-level AUROC/AP by 3.6% on average, and on novel anomaly types it improves zero-shot localization by 5.2%. Evaluated on MVTec-AD, VisA, MPDD, MAD and Real-IAD.

AAAI 2026

Seeing and Knowing in the Wild: Open-domain Visual Entity Recognition with Large-scale Knowledge Graphs via Contrastive Learning

Hongkuan Zhou, Lavdim Halilaj, Sebastian Monka, Stefan Schmid, Yuqicheng Zhu, Jingcheng Wu, Nadeem Nazer, Steffen Staab

AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence (AAAI), 2026

arXiv Proceedings Code

KnowCoL places images and text descriptions in a shared space structured by Wikidata, so the model can recognise entities it never saw in training. On unseen entities in the OVEN benchmark, the smallest model is 10.5% more accurate than the previous state of the art while being 35× smaller.

Preprints

arXiv 2026

GenAU: Language-Grounded Industrial Anomaly Understanding with Vision-Language Models

Hongkuan Zhou, Tristan Rehm, Nadeem Nazer, Lavdim Halilaj, Jingcheng Wu, Steffen Staab

arXiv preprint arXiv:2607.01049, 2026

arXiv PDF

GenAU is a single instruction-following vision-language model for industrial inspection. It handles image-level detection, pixel-level segmentation, multi-type anomaly detection and textual defect analysis together. Dedicated defect and normal segmentation tokens guide localization over multi-scale visual features. Among CLIP-based zero-shot methods it has the strongest image-level detection on VisA and Real-IAD.
